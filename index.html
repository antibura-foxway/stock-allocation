Trade-offs
  • Channel conflict and cannibalization
  • Shares and Shared stock, no warehouse work
  • Reserves, periods and refills come from recalculation
  • The warehouse does not know the shares: counting only
  • Changes in every stock calculation
  • Exact per device, visible to the warehouse
  • Automated warehouse: virtual bins are re-sorted by a scheduled job, no physical move
  • Manual warehouse areas: every rebalance is a physical move
  • No percentages: a Shared bin brings the share problem back
  • Periods and replenishing reserves mean mass moves
